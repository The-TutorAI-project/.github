## About TutorAI

**TutorAI** is an innovative e-learning platform designed to revolutionize learning through:

- **AI-Powered Assistance:** Leveraging cutting-edge AI technology to simplify complex concepts and provide instant support.
- **Comprehensive Learning Tools:** Including chat interactions, flashcards, quizzes, and source-citing compendiums.
- **Curriculum-Based Focus:** Ingest textbooks in various formats and align learning with your specific courses.

Whether you're a student looking to excel academically or a lifelong learner pursuing knowledge, TutorAI is here to guide you every step of the way.
